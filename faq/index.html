01What are Little Red’s opening hours?

The center is listed as open Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. It is closed on Saturdays and Sundays. Contact the center regarding holiday changes.

02Where is Little Red’s Learning Center?

The address is 1319 Westlawn Ln, Prague, OK 74864. You can use the directions link on our Contact page.

03What ages are accepted?

The Oklahoma childcare listing includes infants (0–11 months), toddlers (12–23 months), preschoolers (2–4 years), and school-age children (5 years and older).

04Is Little Red’s open year-round?

Yes. The Oklahoma Human Services childcare listing categorizes this center as a year-round, daytime-hours program.

05How do I find out about available spaces?

For current openings, waiting lists, schedules and enrollment requirements, call (405) 566-8927 or email Littleredsok@gmail.com.

06Can I tour the center or learn about tuition?

Please contact the center directly to discuss arranging a visit, tuition, and its current enrollment process.
